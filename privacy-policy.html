Privacy Policy — Islamic Reel Maker (Deen Studio / Perjalan Hidup)

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 | Meta App ID: 1748707523012139

1. Overview

Islamic Reel Maker (operated by Deen Studio / Perjalan Hidup) is a content publishing and page management integration built on the Meta Graph API. It automates publishing of educational and inspirational short-form videos (Reels) and community engagement on our managed Facebook Pages.

2. Information We Collect and Process

  • Page Metadata & Content: Page ID, Page name, published video/Reel IDs, captions, and public engagement metrics (view counts, likes, and public comments on our own Facebook Pages).
  • No Personal User Profiling: We do not collect, store, sell, or share private personal data of Facebook or Instagram end-users.

3. How We Use Information

Data accessed via the Meta Graph API is used strictly to publish scheduled video content to our own authorized Facebook Pages, display aggregated Page performance analytics, and respond to public comments on our Pages.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or transfer any Meta platform data to third parties. All access tokens and credentials are stored in encrypted environment storage.

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

Islamic Reel Maker does not store personal data of Facebook users. However, if you wish to revoke app permissions or request deletion of any interaction records associated with your account:

  1. Go to your Facebook Account Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites (or Business Integrations) and remove Islamic reel maker.
  2. Alternatively, send an email to hanismathussin@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Islamic Reel Maker". All associated records will be permanently purged within 48 hours.
